    Machine setup
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Do this once, and again when you change the punch or move the machine.
    
        
      
          
    1. Connect the machine. Plug in the USB cable, choose the port and press Connect. Close Candle or UGS first: only one program can use the port.
      2. 
          
    2. Clamp the paper. Lay the sheet flat with its top edge toward the back of the machine, and clamp it so it cannot move.
      3. 
          
    3. Set X/Y zero. Jog the punch tip over the paper's front-left corner (the bottom-left of the page), then press Set X/Y zero here.
      4. 
          
    4. Set Z zero. Lower Z with 1 mm, then 0.1 mm steps until the tip just touches the paper. Press Set Z zero here, then Z+ to lift it again.
      5. 
          
    5. Check the machine values. Punch depth Z is how far below the paper the tip goes: go deeper in small steps until the dots are clear but the paper does not tear. Clearance Z is the height it travels at. Dot, cell and line pitch set the braille spacing.
      6. 
          
    6. Test the start. Choose your paper under 2 Page, then press Go to start position: the tip stops above the first dot, which must be just inside the dashed margin line of the preview on your paper.
      7. 
          
    7. Check the paper. Check in the Tool Box lifts the punch 5 mm and moves it once around the margin line, then back to the start position: the path must stay on your paper, just inside its edges. ■ stops it.
      8. 
          
    8. Lock the values in Machine values, so they do not change by accident.
      9. 
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    These belong to the machine and the punch, not to each print. With Use paper size on, the start position and print area come from the paper size.

    About DotSense
    
      
    DotSense turns everyday text into punched braille on an affordable GRBL 3018 CNC – one complete, offline workflow from words to a tactile page. 
    
      
      
        
    • Three ways inType, dictate with offline voice typing, or scan photos, PDFs and phone pictures with OCR – in English and Bangla.
      • 
        
    • Accurate brailleGrade-1 English and Bangla braille with the Unified English Braille signs – @ # % & ( ) quotes, dashes, money, maths and accented letters – shown at real paper size, with a Unicode braille text of every page for proofreading.
      • 
        
    • Paper-aware layoutA4, Letter and more; the braille size fits the sheet and no dot is ever placed outside its margins.
      • 
        
    • Live printingEvery dot shown as it is punched, a countdown timer, pause, stop and soft reset, mirror printing, and a check mode that tests the G‑code without moving the machine.
      • 
        
    • Guided setupA calibration guide, machine values with a live preview, and a live view of the punch while you jog.
      • 
        
    • Offline and privateVoice typing and OCR run on the computer; nothing is uploaded.
      • 
      
    

      
    
      
    User manual
    
      
    
        
    From an empty text to a punched page in six steps. The chapters below explain every part; the highlighted notes are the ones that matter most.
    
        
      
          
    1. Connect the machine – plug in the USB cable, open Machine setup › Connection, choose the port, 115200 baud, Connect. The status at the top right turns to DotSense connected.
      2. 
          
    2. Calibrate once – follow Machine setup › Calibration guide: X/Y zero at the paper's front-left corner, Z zero on the paper surface.
      3. 
          
    3. Enter the text in Text – type it, use Voice typing, or Scan (OCR) a photo or PDF.
      4. 
          
    4. Choose the paper in Page – switch on Use paper size, pick the size, orientation and margin.
      5. 
          
    5. Check it in Preview – the sheet at its real shape and the Braille text for proofreading.
      6. 
          
    6. Print – clamp the paper, tick Position and paper checked, press Print page or Print all pages.
      7. 
        
    

        
    
          01The window
          
    
            
      
              
    • The bar at the top left: WiFi, Text, Page, Preview, Machine setup, Print, About and the light / dark switch. Text, Page, Preview and Print jump to that part; the part you work in lights up.
      • 
              
    • Top right: the machine status (Not connected, DotSense connected, Printing 42%…; click it for the connection) and Open… / Save… for projects.
      • 
              
    • The Print bar stays at the bottom of the window: machine state and position, pages and time, the safety tick and the print buttons. Messages appear just above it; ✕ closes one.
      • 
              
    • More (under Page) holds Invert print, Between pages, the PDF paper choice and the G-code.
      • 
              
    • Esc closes Machine setup and About. DotSense remembers your text, settings, zoom and theme.
      • 
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          02Entering text
          
    
            
      
              
    • Type or paste into Text. Next page starts a new sheet at the cursor and writes [[New Page 3]] – the number of the page it starts, kept up to date when the text changes. Long text wraps into lines and pages by itself.
      • 
              
    • English / বাংলা sets the language for voice typing. Scanning reads Bangla and English by itself, also mixed in one page.
      • 
              
    • Auto correct (on unless you switch it off) underlines mistakes as you type, in English and Bangla: spelling (sentense → sentence), sentences (he go → he goes, its a → it’s a), punctuation (How are you. → ?, !! → !) and capitals (monday → Monday; আছি. → আছি।). Click an underlined word for its fixes (or right-click it, or Ctrl+.). Ignore skips one, Add to dictionary keeps a name from being marked, Fix all uses the first fix everywhere it is sure. The Show suggestions switch shows or hides the list under the buttons. Clear empties the text box (it asks first).
      • 
              
    • Signs – besides , ; : . ! ? ' -, DotSense punches the Unified English Braille signs: @ # $ % & * + = / \ _ ~ ^ | < > ( ) [ ] { }, “quotes”, dashes – —, money € £ ¥ ¢, © ® ™ § ¶ ° •, maths ± × ÷ ≠ ≤ ≥ ≡ ∫ ∑ ∈ ⊂ ∩ ∪ ½ ¾ ² ³, arrows ← → ⇒, shapes ■ ○, Greek α β π Ω, and accented letters such as é ñ ü ç ø ß æ. Point at a cell in the preview to see what it is.
      • 
              
    • Voice typing – the first time, choose a speech model to download once (Accurate 111 MB, Fast 30 MB, Bangla 87 MB); after that it works offline. Say “new line”, “new paragraph” or “new page” (Bangla: নতুন লাইন, নতুন অনুচ্ছেদ, নতুন পাতা).
      • 
              
    • Scan (OCR)… reads PNG, JPG, BMP, WebP and PDF files (or drop them on the window) – Bangla and English, also mixed, with the models that come with the app. Photos from a camera or the phone are cleaned up first (shadows and uneven light taken out) and the lines of each paragraph are joined. Choose Replace or Add after, a PDF page range (last page blank = to the end), OCR every PDF page for scanned PDFs, a new sheet for each source page, or Keep the picture's line breaks for poems and lists.
      • 
              
    • Photo from phone (in Scan) – press Use phone camera, put the phone on the same WiFi (the machine's WiFi MKS_DLC; WiFi at the top left joins it in one click), scan the code and tap Take photo. On the phone each photo waits with Send, Crop (drag the frame's corners or edges) and Remove; after Send the text goes straight into the text box. Click a small photo in Scan to see it large – Remove photo there also takes its text out.
      • 
            
    
            
    ImportantAlways read the text after scanning or dictating: OCR and speech can mishear or misread. Characters that are not in the braille table are listed in a warning and punched as blank cells.
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          03Page and paper
          
    
            
      
              
    • Switch on Use paper size and pick the sheet (A4, Letter, A5, envelopes… or User-Defined), Portrait or Landscape, and a Margin in cm or mm.
      • 
              
    • Braille size: Fit to printer margins makes the braille fill one sheet (50–200 %), Reduce to printer margins only shrinks it, Custom scale is your own size.
      • 
              
    • The dots stay 2 mm inside the margin line, and DotSense checks every dot again before the machine moves: nothing is punched outside the page.
      • 
              
    • If the sheet is bigger than the machine can reach (a typical 3018 moves about 300 × 180 mm), a warning offers the fix as a button – for example Landscape with a 28 mm margin.
      • 
              
    • With Use paper size off, your own start position and print area from Machine values are used.
      • 
            
    
            
    ImportantDotSense measures the paper from X0 Y0 at its front-left corner. If the size or braille size changes the start position, the safety tick is cleared so you check it again.
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          04Preview and proofreading
          
    
            
      
              
    • The preview shows the sheet at its real shape with every dot where it will be punched. Fit page, 100 % and 200 % zoom; ‹ › change the page.
      • 
              
    • The numbers: letters, dots, lines and pages. The Print bar says how long the text takes; while printing its progress line counts down the time left (00:00:00 left).
      • 
              
    • Braille text under the sheet is the page in Unicode braille, line by line – for proofreading, braille displays and screen readers. Copy puts it on the clipboard.
      • 
              
    • Invert view puts the mirrored machine side next to the reading side; ↗ opens it in its own window.
      • 
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          05Machine setup and calibration
          
    
            
      
              
    • Calibration guide – eight steps from connecting to locking the values, with a button to each tab.
      • 
              
    • Machine values – start position, print area, dot / cell / line pitch, clearance Z, punch depth Z, plunge feed and settle dwell. They are locked: switch the lock off to change them and on again afterwards. The Live preview shows the sheet, the spacing and the Z heights as you type.
      • 
              
    • Tool Box – jog in steps of 0.1, 1 or 10 mm (or cm), Set X/Y zero here, Set Z zero here, Go to start position, Unlock, Soft Reset and Check (auto calibrate: 5 mm up, once around the margin line, back to the start). Live position shows where the punch is, as the machine reports it.
      • 
              
    • Connection – port, baud rate, Connect / Disconnect, the machine log and a command line.
      • 
            
    
            
    ImportantFind the punch depth in small steps: deep enough for clear dots, not so deep that the paper tears. Test with Go to start position before the first real print.
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          06Printing
          
    
            
      
              
    1. Clamp the paper and tick Position and paper checked. Hover a greyed-out Print button to see what is still missing.
      2. 
              
    2. Print page N or Print all pages, then confirm.
      3. 
              
    3. Watch it live: punched dots turn dark, the pulsing dot is the one being punched, the time left counts down in the Print bar.
      4. 
              
    4. Pause holds the machine (Resume goes on); Stop holds, resets and lifts the punch to clearance.
      5. 
              
    5. With several pages DotSense stops after each sheet and beeps; the next page starts by itself after 30 s (More › Between pages). Continue now, Wait or Stop here.
      6. 
            
    
            
      
              
    • Invert print (More, on by default) punches the page mirrored, so it reads correctly from the back.
      • 
              
    • Check (Tool Box) lifts the punch 5 mm and moves it once around the margin line and back to the start, so you can see that the paper is in the right place before punching.
      • 
              
    • Soft Reset aborts a job at once; afterwards press Unlock, lift the punch with Z+ and check the position.
      • 
            
    
            
    ImportantPut the next sheet in at exactly the same place (same work zero) before the countdown ends.
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          07Projects and files
          
    
            
      
              
    • Save… stores the text and all settings as a DotSense project; Open… brings it back (Braille Gcode 2 projects open too).
      • 
              
    • More › G-code: Copy, Save page… (.gcode) and Save all (ZIP)… for other senders. With Invert print the file names end in _invert.
      • 
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          08Troubleshooting
          
    
            
      
              
    • Port busy / access denied – close Candle, UGS or the Arduino IDE: only one program can use the port.
      • 
              
    • No port listed (Windows) – install the CH340 USB driver for the board.
      • 
              
    • No reply from GRBL – check the baud rate (115200) and the USB cable.
      • 
              
    • ALARM – check the machine, then Unlock; after a limit alarm set zero again.
      • 
              
    • The sheet is out of reach – use the fix button in Page (Landscape or a bigger margin).
      • 
              
    • The phone cannot open the page – same WiFi on both; allow DotSense in Windows Firewall (also Public networks); on Android turn mobile data off.
      • 
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          09Safety
          
    
            
    ImportantStay at the machine while it prints. Stop in the software is not an emergency stop: keep a hand near the power switch.
    
            
      
              
    • Keep your hands away from the punch, above all while the countdown between pages runs.
      • 
              
    • Clamp the paper so it cannot move; check the start position and Z depth for every new setup.
      • 
              
    • Text and settings are locked while printing, so nothing changes under the running job.
